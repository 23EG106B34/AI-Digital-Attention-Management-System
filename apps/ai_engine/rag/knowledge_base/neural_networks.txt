A neural network is a machine-learning model inspired by the brain.
It consists of layers of interconnected units (neurons): an input
layer, one or more hidden layers, and an output layer. Each connection
has a weight; training adjusts weights via backpropagation to minimize
a loss function. Activation functions such as ReLU or sigmoid introduce
non-linearity. Neural networks underpin deep learning for image,
speech, and language tasks.
